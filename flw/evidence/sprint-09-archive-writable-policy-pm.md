# Sprint09 PM actual archive writable-replacement refinement vote

Voter: PM /root/pm_s9. Vote: **FOR** the exact refinement received from Root: when initiating owner archives their current project, require another active project in which that owner can write; prefer that writable replacement for all sessions of the initiator; if the only other membership is Viewer, refuse before mutation/audit and explain that the owner must first create/select an active writable project. Other affected viewers may be rebound to their own accessible Viewer project. Do not weaken the current-project POST writer gate.

Therefore a current owner can archive their writable project and land in a Viewer workspace unable to restore/create/delete. Source inspection independently confirms the lockout mechanism; no runtime scenario was executed by this PM.

The refinement preserves the original safe lifecycle/session and role contract, strengthens the agreed replacement policy, and keeps Viewer access read-only. It does not require every affected viewer to own a writable project. Independent QA must prove: only-Viewer refusal leaves complete data/audit/revision unchanged; writable alternate allows archive and follow-up restore/create/delete; initiator sessions consistently select writable replacements; other users do not gain roles or foreign access; stale CAS and restart remain safe.
